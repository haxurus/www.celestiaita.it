Help the community

Help us grow Celestia!

Ambassador

Represent the community.

Ambassadors are not staff members, but they help Celestia by creating and opening public or group lobbies on behalf of the community.

  • Open lobbies on behalf of Celestia.
  • Help the community stay more visible and active on VRChat.
  • You do not have staff moderation responsibilities.
Open a Discord ticket VRChat Ambassador Group
Staffer

Join the staff team.

Staff members are directly involved in managing the community and actively moderate the group's official lobbies.

  • Actively moderate Celestia lobbies.
  • Help maintain an organized, respectful, and welcoming environment.
  • Work with the rest of the staff to manage the community.
Open the application form

To become an Ambassador, you can open a ticket on Discord or request to join the dedicated VRChat group. Staff applications are handled through the dedicated application form.
